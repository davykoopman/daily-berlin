Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. Any updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be adjusted accordingly.

How We Collect and Use Personal Information

To operate and improve our Services, we collect personal information from various sources. We use this data to communicate with you, process orders, meet legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect both our customers and our business.

What Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use our Services, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address
  • Billing and payment-related information (handled securely by our payment providers)
  • Order and transaction details
  • Messages sent to our customer support team

Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our Website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser details
  • Pages visited and interaction data
  • Cookie and tracking information

This data helps us improve performance and user experience.

Information From Third Parties

We may receive limited information from trusted partners such as:

  • Payment processors
  • Shipping and logistics providers
  • Analytics and marketing platforms

These parties only receive data necessary to perform their services.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Communicate about purchases, returns, or support requests
  • Provide customer service
  • Improve our Website and Services
  • Send marketing communications where legally permitted
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enable core website functions, analyse performance, and improve user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Website.

Sharing of Information

We may share personal data only when necessary with:

  • Service providers supporting our operations
  • Payment and delivery partners
  • Analytics and marketing providers
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal data.

Third-Party Websites

Our Website may contain references to external websites not operated by us. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Security & Retention

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information. While no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, we work to safeguard your data against unauthorised access.

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill orders
  • Meet legal, tax, or accounting requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

  • Access your personal data

  • Restrict or object to processing

To exercise your rights, please contact us at: support@alovefashion.com
