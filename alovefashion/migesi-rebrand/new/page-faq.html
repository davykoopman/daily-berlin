Welcome to the Migesi FAQ page.

Here you'll find answers to the most common questions about our products, delivery, returns, and customer support. If you need further assistance, our team is always here to help.

How Long Does Delivery Take?

Our estimated delivery times are as follows:

  • Order cut-off time: 5:00 PM (GMT+00:00). Orders placed after this time are processed on the next business day.
  • Order processing: 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays)
  • Shipping time: 6–9 business days (Monday–Friday)
  • Total estimated delivery: 7–11 business days

Please note that these are estimated timeframes and may vary slightly due to courier delays or busy periods. For more details, please refer to our Shipping Policy.

Do You Offer Free Shipping?

Yes, we offer free shipping on all orders within the United Kingdom, unless stated otherwise during checkout.

Can I Cancel My Order?

If your order has not yet been processed, you may request a cancellation by contacting us as soon as possible.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible and the return process will apply.

What Is Your Return Policy?

You have the right to return your order within 14 days of delivery.

  • Items must be unworn and in original condition
  • Returns are free of charge
  • Returns must be sent using a tracked shipping method

Full details can be found in our Returns Policy.

When Will I Receive My Refund?

After your return has been received and inspected:

  • Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Your payment provider may take additional time to complete the transaction
