Returns and Refunds

At Davento, we want every purchase to be perfect for your wardrobe. We know that finding the right style or fit sometimes takes a second try, which is why we want shopping with us to be completely stress-free. Browse our collections with peace of mind, knowing we are here to help at every step.

60 days to return

You have up to 60 days from the day your parcel is delivered to request a return. No reason needed.

For a full refund, clothing and accessories must be in their original condition: unworn, unwashed and returned with all original labels and the protective packaging they arrived in.

We do not charge a restocking fee. You get back the full price of the item, with no hidden costs or admin charges.

How to start a return

Starting a return is easy. Simply email support@davento.it with your order number and the items you would like to return. Our team will reply promptly with everything you need to send your parcel back.

Return shipping costs are paid by the customer. We strongly recommend using a tracked shipping service, so you can follow your parcel at all times and be sure it reaches our warehouse safely.

Exchanges

If you simply need a different size or would like to swap an item for another style, exchanges are always available on request. Just mention it in your email to support@davento.it and we will arrange it together with you.

Refund times

Once we receive your parcel, our team will check the items. We process your refund within 7 working days of the check, and always within 14 days of receiving the items back.

The refund is made to the payment method you used for your order. Please note that your bank or card provider may need a few extra working days before the amount shows in your account.

Your statutory rights

Our 60-day return period is in addition to your rights under UK consumer law, not instead of them.

  • Right to cancel: under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you can cancel your order within 14 days from the day after delivery, without giving a reason. You then have another 14 days to send the items back. To cancel, send us a clear statement by email, for example using the cancellation form on our Returns page. If an item has been handled more than needed to check it (as you would in a shop), we may reduce the refund by the loss in value.
  • Faulty or wrong items: under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, items must be as described, of satisfactory quality and fit for purpose. If you receive a faulty, damaged or wrong item, please email us with your order number and a photo. In that case we pay the return costs, and you can choose a replacement or a refund.

We are here for you

Your satisfaction is our top priority. If you have any questions, doubts or would like advice on fit before making an exchange, do not hesitate to write to us at support@davento.it. We are always happy to help you find the perfect look.
