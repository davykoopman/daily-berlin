Shipping and Delivery

At Davento, we believe waiting for your new clothes should feel as good as wearing them. That is why our delivery process is simple, clear and free of surprises, designed to bring your favourite pieces straight to your door with complete peace of mind.

Shipping is free on every order. We deliver to all countries you can select at checkout, and every parcel is prepared and checked with great care before it is handed to the courier.

Shipping times

To help you plan your purchases, this is how our handling and delivery times work:

  • Processing time: your order is usually prepared, packed and handed to the courier within 1–2 working days.

  • Transit time: once shipped, your parcel normally takes 3 to 7 working days to reach you.

  • Total delivery time: in most cases, you will receive your order within 4 to 9 working days in total.

Our cut-off time for same-day processing is 17:00 Central European Time (16:00 UK time), Monday to Saturday. Orders received after this time are processed from the next working day.

Tracking and support

As soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse, we will send you a confirmation email with your tracking number and the courier link, so you can follow your order step by step.

We always do everything we can to meet the delivery times above. In rare cases, there may be small delays due to seasonal peaks, logistics issues or bad weather. If this happens, we will let you know promptly with an update on your delivery.

If you need help, have a question about your parcel or would like personal support, our team is happy to help. You can email us at any time at support@davento.it and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
