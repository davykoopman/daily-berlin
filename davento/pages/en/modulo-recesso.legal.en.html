Right to cancel
Under UK consumer law (the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), you can cancel your purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. With Davento, you even have 60 days.

Important: the cancellation period starts on the day your parcel is delivered, as shown by the tracking status "delivered". You can of course also tell us before delivery that you no longer want your order.

How to cancel:

  • You can cancel within 60 days from the day of delivery.
  • Within this period, email us at support@davento.it with your order number and a clear statement that you want to cancel. You can use the form below, but you do not have to.
  • Return shipping costs are paid by the customer. For faulty, damaged or wrong items, we pay the return costs.

For a full refund, the item must:

  • not have been worn or used
  • be in its original condition
  • come with all original labels and packaging

If an item has been handled more than needed to check it, we may reduce the refund by the loss in value. Once we have received and checked your return, we refund you within 7 working days, and always within 14 days of receiving it. Your bank or payment provider may need some extra time before the amount shows in your account.
