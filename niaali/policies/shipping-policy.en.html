Fashion can change your mood in an instant. At Niaali, we make sure that the journey from clicking “order” to trying on your new garment is just as pleasant as the purchase itself. Our shipping process has been designed to be transparent, efficient, and predictable.

We deliver to all countries available at checkout. Each order is carefully packed, with attention to detail, before it leaves our warehouse.

Shipping is free on all orders, with no minimum order value.

Processing and delivery times

  • Processing time: Orders are prepared for shipment within 1–2 business days.
  • Transit time: Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days from the date of dispatch.
  • Total delivery time: In most cases, the shipment arrives within 4–9 business days.

The order cut-off time is 14:00 Polish time (Warsaw), Monday through Saturday.

Once your parcel has been dispatched, you will receive an email with a tracking number, allowing you to check the current location of your shipment.

We make every effort to ensure that orders arrive as planned. In exceptional circumstances, such as an increased number of orders or unforeseen logistical difficulties, delivery may take longer. In such cases, we provide ongoing updates on the status of your shipment.

Do you have questions about shipping? Contact us at any time at support@niaali.com. We will be happy to help.
