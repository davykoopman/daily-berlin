What is Niaali?
Niaali offers carefully selected women's and men's fashion, combining style, comfort and the unique character of each collection, so that everyone can express themselves and enjoy an exceptional shopping experience.

Do you offer free shipping?
Yes, shipping is free on all orders, with no minimum order amount and no hidden conditions. Every parcel comes with a tracking number. We believe shopping should be simple and stress-free.

How long does delivery take?
Orders are prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days. Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch, so in most cases your parcel arrives within 4–9 business days. Once it has shipped, you will receive an email with your tracking number.

Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship to Poland, the Netherlands and the United Kingdom. Our goal is to provide the best possible customer service. If we expand our shipping coverage in the future, we will let you know first.

What is your return policy?
Changed your mind? No problem. You have 60 days from the delivery date to request a return. The item must be unused, in its original condition and with complete packaging. Email us at support@niaali.com with your order number and we will guide you through the process. Return shipping costs are paid by the customer.

How long does it take to receive a refund?
Once we have received and checked the returned item, we issue your refund within 7 business days. Depending on your bank, the funds may appear in your account slightly later.

Can I exchange a product?
Yes, we offer exchanges for a different size or model. Email us at support@niaali.com and we will help you choose the right option.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept BLIK, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, Klarna and Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express and UnionPay cards. Available methods may vary by country. All payments are processed securely using SSL encryption.

Are my personal and payment details safe?
Of course. Privacy protection and payment security are our top priorities. Niaali never stores your card details, and the entire payment process is encrypted.

What should I do if my order has not arrived?
First, check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email. If 9 business days have passed since you placed your order and the parcel still hasn't arrived, email us at support@niaali.com and we will look into it right away.

Can I change or cancel my order?
If your order has not yet been shipped, we may be able to change or cancel it. Contact us as soon as possible after placing your order, and we will do everything we can to help.

Still need help?
We are here for you. Email us at support@niaali.com and our team will be happy to help. We reply Monday to Friday from 8am to 5pm and at weekends from 9am to 4pm (CET).
