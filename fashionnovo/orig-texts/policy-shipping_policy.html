At Fashionnovo, we believe that waiting for your new fashion pieces should be just as enjoyable as wearing them. That's why we've designed a shipping process that is simple, transparent, and hassle-free, ensuring your favorite items arrive safely at your doorstep.

We cover all shipping costs within UK, so the price you see at checkout is the final price you'll pay—there are no hidden fees or additional shipping charges. At this time, we only ship to addresses within UK. Every order is carefully prepared, inspected, and packaged before being handed over to our shipping partner.

Shipping Timeframes

To help you plan your purchase, here's what you can expect:

Processing Time
Orders are prepared, packed, and dispatched within 1–3 business days.

Transit Time
Once shipped, delivery typically takes 3–8 business days.

Estimated Total Delivery Time
In most cases, your order will arrive within 4–11 business days from the date it is placed.

Our daily order processing cut-off time is 5:00 PM (All times are UK time (BST, GMT+1)), Monday through Saturday. Orders placed after this time will begin processing on the next business day.

Order Tracking & Support

As soon as your order leaves our warehouse, you'll receive a confirmation email containing your tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page, allowing you to follow your package every step of the way.

While we always strive to meet the estimated delivery times above, occasional delays may occur due to seasonal demand, logistics disruptions, or adverse weather conditions. If this happens, we'll keep you informed with timely updates regarding the status of your shipment.

If you have any questions about your order or need assistance tracking your package, our customer support team is here to help. Feel free to contact us anytime at support@fashionnovo.com, and we'll be happy to assist you as quickly as possible.
