Welcome to the Fashionnovo Help Center. We've gathered the most common questions here to provide clear answers in one place. If you still need assistance after reading, our team is just an email away.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes. We offer free shipping on every order within the United Kingdom, with no minimum purchase required.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, and UnionPay. Every payment is secured with SSL encryption.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. After shipping, delivery typically takes an additional 3–8 business days, for a total of approximately 4-11 business days. As soon as your order ships, you'll receive a tracking email.

What is Fashionnovo?

Fashionnovo is an online fashion store built around carefully curated clothing that combines style, comfort, and lasting quality, making it easy to dress well every day.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

If your order has not yet been shipped, please contact us as soon as possible at support@fashionnovo.com, and we'll do our best to assist you.

Do you ship internationally?

At the moment, we only deliver within the United Kingdom.

What is your return policy?

You have 60 days from the date of delivery to request a return. Simply email us at support@fashionnovo.com, and we'll guide you through the process step by step.

How long do refunds take?

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll process your refund within 7 business days. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take a few additional days for the refund to appear in your account.

Do you offer exchanges?

Yes. If you need a different size or would like to exchange your item for another product, contact us at support@fashionnovo.com, and we'll arrange the exchange.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are protected with SSL encryption, and your card details are never stored on our servers.

My order hasn't arrived yet. What should I do?

First, check the tracking link included in your shipping confirmation email. If something doesn't seem right, contact us at support@fashionnovo.com, and we'll be happy to help.

Still have a question?

Feel free to contact us anytime at support@fashionnovo.com. Our team is always happy to help you find what you need.
