Paying for your order should be as simple and secure as the rest of your experience with Fashionnovo. On this page, we explain how payments work and how your information is protected.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, UnionPay, PayPal and Klarna. All orders are charged in GBP.

The full amount will be charged at the time of placing your order. In some cases, your bank may display a temporary authorization hold before the final charge is processed. This is a completely normal part of the payment process.

Every payment is protected with SSL encryption, and your card details are never stored on our servers. To ensure your payment is processed successfully, please make sure your billing information matches the details registered with your chosen payment method.

Occasionally, a transaction may be subject to verification as part of our standard fraud prevention checks. This is simply a security measure to protect you and rarely causes delays.

If you experience any issues during checkout, please contact us at support@fashionnovo.com, and we will help you complete your order as smoothly as possible.
